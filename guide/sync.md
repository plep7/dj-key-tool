# Sync to other apps

Sync sends your playlists to another app's library, so you can prep here and play there.
You'll find **Sync to Serato, Traktor or Engine...** in the **Tracks** menu.
If you have no playlists yet, the table's tracks go over as one list.

**It's new.**
It has been checked against real files from all three apps.
But no copy of Serato, Traktor or Engine DJ has opened a synced library yet.
So try it on a small playlist first.

## What goes over

| App | What goes | What stays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traktor | New tracks with tags, rating, key, BPM, grid and cues, and playlists | Every track already there, with its grid and tags |
| Serato | New tracks and crates, and with the option ticked, cues, written into the music files because that is where Serato keeps them | Your crates, tracks and their grids |
| Engine DJ (2.2 to 5.0) | New tracks with tags, rating, key, grid, cues, loops and an overview waveform, and playlists | Every track already there, with its grid and tags |

## How it keeps your library safe

- **You see it first.**
  Picking an app lists every change, and nothing gets written until you press **Sync**.
- **Close the other app first.**
  A sync is refused while the other app is open.
  All three apps write their library back when they close, which would wipe out what the sync wrote.
- **Your lists stay yours.**
  Everything goes into a folder named Pat In Key.
  Deleting a playlist here takes nothing out of the other app.
- **You can undo it.**
  Everything the sync can touch gets backed up first.
  **Undo last sync...** in the dialog puts the app's library back.
  If the app changed its library since, you're told first, because those changes go too.
